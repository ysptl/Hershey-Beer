    Kegs & more

    Throwing a party?

    We do more than ring up 30-racks. Call ahead for anything on this list.

    • Kegs

      Kegs for any size crowd. Call ahead and we'll make sure yours is ready. A standard half-barrel pours about 165 twelve-ounce beers.

    • CO₂ refills

      Tank exchanges and refills for your kegerator or draft setup.

    • Taps & tubs

      Rent a tap and a tub for parties, tailgates and events.

    • Special orders

      Looking for something we don't stock? Tell us what it is and we'll try to get it in.

    • Delivery

      Order online through our DoorDash page and get it brought to your door. Have your ID ready, since the driver checks it at drop-off.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Can I buy just one can?

    Yep. We sell singles, and you can put six of them together in a Mix-A-Six.

    Do I need to call ahead for a keg?

    It's a good idea, especially for weekends and holidays. Call 717-566-8231 and we'll set one aside.

    Can you get a beer you don't have?

    We take special orders for hard-to-find beers and kegs. Stop in or call and tell us what you're after.

    Do you card?

    Every time. You need to be 21 or older with a valid photo ID to buy alcohol, tobacco or vape products.
