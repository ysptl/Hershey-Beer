The short version

We don't collect personal information through this website. There are no accounts, sign-up forms, ads or tracking cookies. The only thing the site saves is a note on your own device that you confirmed you're 21, so you don't get asked again.

01What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hershey Beer website ("the site"), run by Hershey Beer, 659 E. Main Street, Hummelstown, PA 17036 ("we," "us" or "our"). It does not cover information you share with us in our store, or information collected by other companies whose websites we link to.

02Information we collect

We do not ask for or collect personal information through the site. The site has no accounts, contact forms, newsletter sign-ups or checkout.

If you choose to call or email us, we'll receive whatever you share, like your name, phone number, email address and what you're asking about. We use that only to answer you and help with your request.

03Your age confirmation

When you confirm that you're 21 or older, the site saves a small note in your browser's local storage so it doesn't ask you again on that device. This note stays on your device. It isn't sent to us, and it doesn't contain your name, birthday or any other personal details.

You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website. You'll be asked to confirm your age again the next time you visit.

04Information collected by our service providers

Like most websites, the site relies on a few outside services to work. These services may automatically receive basic technical information when your browser loads the site:

  • Website hosting (Netlify). Our host may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The site loads its fonts from Google, which receives your IP address and browser information when the fonts load.

We don't use these services to identify you, and we don't run analytics, advertising or tracking tools on the site.

05Cookies

The site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. The only item it stores in your browser is the age confirmation note described above.

06Links to other services

The site links to services run by other companies, including DoorDash for online orders and Google Maps for directions. When you use them, their own privacy policies apply, and any information you give them, such as delivery address or payment details on DoorDash, goes to them, not to us through this site.

07How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We only share information you give us if we need to in order to help you, or if we are required to by law.

08People under 21

The site is intended only for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21, and anyone under 18 should not use the site.

09Security

The site is served over a secure HTTPS connection. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, but since the site does not collect personal information, there is very little information to protect.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the site changes, for example if we add a contact form or online features. When we do, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
